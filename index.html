THE YEAR WE SHARED

Our 2026 Highlights

Personal reflections and memorable experiences from our four team members.

01 / JOSHUA

Joshua Brown

As I prepare to graduate in December 2026, I am reflecting on my six-year college journey and everything I have learned along the way. This experience has taught me the importance of persistence, discipline, and consistency. Despite the challenges I have faced, I have continued moving forward, strengthening my skills, and preparing for a career in software engineering. I celebrate 2026 as a year of personal growth, perseverance, and the beginning of a new chapter in my life.

02 / VALERIA

Valeria Rondon

This year has not been easy, but I have learned a lot along the way. I am celebrating 2026 through my graduation. This year, I worked on projects with many wonderful teammates in my classes, which helped me improve my cooperation and programming skills. Occasionally, I was unsure whether I could finish my assignments on time. My anxiety also got the best of me while preparing for tests, but I was able to push through. The year is not over yet, and we all have to keep going until the end to celebrate.

03 / JUAN

Juan Sanchez

I celebrate 2026 by reflecting on all the new experiences I have had throughout this year, each of which has helped me become a more mature and better version of myself. These experiences have taught me valuable lessons that will guide my future decisions, reminding me that personal growth often comes from learning from our mistakes. If someone had told me a year ago everything I would experience in 2026, I would have found it difficult to believe.

Most importantly, this year marked a significant turning point in my life, as I finally experienced what it feels like to live almost entirely free from anxiety. The sense of freedom and peace that came with it is difficult to put into words. Without anxiety holding me back as it once did, I was able to embrace new opportunities, enjoy experiences more fully, and face challenges with greater confidence. For the first time in a long while, I could truly recognize how much I had grown, not only as a professional but also as a person.

04 / CORRAN

Corran Chastain

I'm celebrating 2026 by reflecting on all the experiences I've gained throughout the year. This year has meant a lot to me because I created memorable experiences with my friends, including a trip to Garner State Park.

I also made the decision to leave my job after five years so I could focus fully on college and make sure I'm learning everything I need to know to be successful in life.

Although the year isn't over yet, I personally believe 2026 has been a very successful year for me, and I intend to end it on a good note.
