About Me

Hello, my name is Joshua Brown. I am a Computer Science student at the University of Houston-Downtown, currently completing my final semester and preparing to graduate in December 2026. My college journey has taken several years, and throughout that experience I have learned a great deal about persistence, discipline, and continuing to move forward even when something is challenging. I chose computer science because I genuinely enjoy the creative process of building something from an idea and turning it into something functional that people can actually use.

My main interests are software engineering, front-end development, and full-stack web development. I have worked with technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, and React, and I have been applying those skills by building my own web projects. One of the things I enjoy most about programming is seeing a project develop step by step. Starting with an idea, working through the problems, making improvements, and eventually seeing the finished product is what makes computer science rewarding to me. As I finish college, I am continuing to strengthen my technical skills and prepare for a professional career in software engineering.

Outside of computer science, music has been an important part of my life. I participated in band throughout school and served as a drum major during my senior year of high school. That experience helped shape the way I approach leadership, responsibility, communication, and working toward a goal. I also enjoy technology, movies, gaming, and learning new things. I consider myself someone who values personal growth, independence, and doing things with purpose. As I move into the next chapter of my life, my goal is to continue growing as a software engineer, build meaningful projects, and create a successful career doing work that I genuinely enjoy.
